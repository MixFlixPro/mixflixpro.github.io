Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

[NodaGroup] built WAFI PLAYER as a free, ad-supported media player. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use the application.

By using WAFI PLAYER, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About WAFI PLAYER

WAFI PLAYER is an independent media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV devices.

The application does not provide, host, sell, or distribute television channels, subscriptions, movies, series, playlists, or copyrighted content.

Users are responsible for the local files, URLs, playlists, and other playback information they add to the application and must have the legal right or authorization to access and play such content.

2. Local Media Files

When you choose a local video or audio file, WAFI PLAYER accesses the selected file only to play it on your device.

Selected local media files are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

The application may use Android's system file picker to allow you to select media from device storage or a connected storage device.

3. User-Added URLs and Playlists

Users may enter media URLs, playlist URLs, playlist names, playback headers, cookies, referrer values, origin values, custom user-agent values, and DRM license information.

This information is processed only to provide the playback functionality requested by the user.

Saved playlists, application preferences, and related settings may be stored locally on the user's device until they are deleted from the application, the application's storage is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

When a user starts playback, the relevant URL and required request information may be transmitted to the selected media server, playlist server, DRM license server, or other destination chosen by the user.

These external servers may receive technical information normally transmitted during an internet connection, including the user's IP address, user-agent information, request headers, and connection time.

Users should not enter passwords, authentication tokens, cookies, or other sensitive information unless they trust the destination server and are authorized to use that information.

4. Peer-to-Peer Playback

Where a peer-to-peer playback feature is enabled, network connection information, including IP address and connection metadata, may be exchanged with other peers as required to establish the connection.

Peer-to-peer features are used only when enabled for the relevant playback source.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

WAFI PLAYER uses third-party services and software development kits to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, detect fraud, and maintain application functionality.

These services may include:

• Google Play Services
• Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob
• Google Interactive Media Ads SDK
• Wortise

Depending on their configuration and the user's device settings, these providers may collect or process information such as:

• IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
• Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers
• Device model, operating system, language, and application version
• Application launches, taps, video views, and advertising interactions
• Crash, diagnostic, performance, and technical information
• Network and connection information
• Information about advertisements displayed or interacted with

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

The developer does not control every data-processing activity performed independently by third-party advertising providers.

6. Log and Diagnostic Data

If an error occurs, third-party SDKs integrated into WAFI PLAYER may process diagnostic information relating to the application or device.

This may include application version, operating system version, device model, application launch information, crash details, performance information, timestamps, and other technical statistics.

This information may be used for application stability, advertising measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WAFI PLAYER does not directly operate a website-based cookie system inside the application.

However, advertising providers, external media servers, or links opened by the user may use cookies, advertising identifiers, local storage, or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

WAFI PLAYER uses reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the application.

HTTPS and encrypted connections are used where supported by the destination service.

Some user-provided or third-party media sources may use unencrypted HTTP connections. Information transmitted through an HTTP connection is not protected in the same way as information transmitted through HTTPS.

Users should avoid sending sensitive information to untrusted or unencrypted servers.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not maintain an account database for WAFI PLAYER and does not store users' local media files on developer-operated servers.

Locally saved playlists, URLs, and settings remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears the application's storage, or uninstalls the application.

Data processed independently by third-party providers is retained according to their respective privacy policies and legal requirements.

Users may contact the developer using the email address below regarding privacy questions or deletion requests relating to information controlled directly by the developer.

10. External Links and Services

WAFI PLAYER may open user-provided URLs, media servers, advertisement links, or other third-party services.

These third-party services are not operated or controlled by the developer. Users should review the privacy policies and security practices of any external service they access.

The developer is not responsible for the content, availability, privacy practices, or security of third-party services.

11. Children's Privacy

WAFI PLAYER is not directed to children under the age of 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law.

The developer does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please contact the developer so that appropriate action can be taken.
